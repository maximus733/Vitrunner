How Fulfillment Works

Three-step verification from store shelf to hostel main gate.

01

Post Basket

Select items from the V-Mart catalog (minimum ₹50). Your hostel block sets your delivery fee and zone automatically.

02

Receipt Verification

A runner in your hostel zone purchases the items at V-Mart and uploads a photo of the printed receipt before heading to your gate.

03

UPI & OTP Handoff

Pay the exact bill plus distance fee to the runner's UPI ID at the gate, then provide your 4-digit OTP to close the order.
